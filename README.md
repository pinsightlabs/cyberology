# Cyberology

> Still Learning. Still Training. Still Defending.

This is my catch-all repo for the cybersecurity work I'm learning and teaching. It's a growing, hands-on knowledge base built from the ground up. I keep my notes, references, and study material here so they stay in one place, and I'm sharing them publicly in case they help you too. Feel free to use anything here in your own classroom or your own prep.

Everything is written as study material for learning and for authorized systems only. I'm learning right alongside anyone reading this, so if something is wrong or unclear, I'd genuinely like to hear it. Open an issue and tell me what works for you.

## Command Line

The first complete section. It walks through `cmd.exe` one topic at a time, and the files are numbered so they read like a short course.

| # | Note | What it covers |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | [Command Prompt Basics](command-line/01-command-prompt-basics.md) | What `cmd.exe` is, how to open it, local vs. remote access, and reading the shell. |
| 02 | [Getting Help](command-line/02-getting-help.md) | The built-in `help` system, the `/?` switch, command history, and clearing the screen. |
| 03 | [System Navigation](command-line/03-system-navigation.md) | Moving around with `cd`, absolute vs. relative paths, and mapping folders with `tree`. |
| 04 | [Files and Directories](command-line/04-files-and-directories.md) | Creating, viewing, copying, moving, and deleting files and folders, plus I/O redirection. |
| 05 | [Gathering System Information](command-line/05-gathering-system-info.md) | Host enumeration with `systeminfo`, `ipconfig`, `whoami`, and the `net` commands. |

Keep the [quick reference](command-line/cmd-quick-reference.md) open while you practice. It gathers every command in the section onto one page.
