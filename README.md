## What This Is

A career pathway that takes a learner from no background into cybersecurity, one step at a time. Every step is built around training that is free to enroll in and complete, so cost is never the barrier. Paid certification exams and optional books are separate from the free path.

Each step is its own document with the courses, hands-on practice, skill demonstrations, and completion evidence for that stage.

## How to Use It

- Start at Step 1 and work the learning areas in order.
- Use the completion checklist at the end of each step to confirm you are ready to move on.
- The free courses are the required path. Certifications are a goal to work toward, not a gate to the next step.
