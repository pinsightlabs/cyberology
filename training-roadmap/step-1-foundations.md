## Purpose

Anyone with aspirations of a career in cybersecurity needs a solid technical foundation. Before identifying threats, preventing attacks, and responding to security incidents, they must first understand how the technology they are responsible for protecting works.

This first stage builds computer hardware, operating system, software, networking, and troubleshooting knowledge through free instruction and practical demonstrations. Learners finish Step 1 with evidence of real skills, not just completed videos.

## Who This Is For

- High school students
- College students
- Adult career changers
- Independent learners

**What you will earn:** Documented skill demonstrations in five learning areas, readiness to compare your skills against the official CompTIA A+ objectives, and a launch point into Step 2, Networking Knowledge & Skills.

## Training Standard

Required initial courses must be free to enroll in and complete, including the instructional content and required learning activities. Platform usage limits may apply. Paid certification examinations and optional books are separate from the free learning pathway.

## 1 | Computer Hardware

**Goal:** Recognize physical computer components, explain their purpose, and understand how the components work together.

| Course | Provider | Duration | Access | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Computer Hardware Basics | Cisco Networking Academy | ~6 hrs | Free | Required |
| Introduction to Computers and Computer Systems | Open University / OpenLearn | ~16 hrs | Free | Required |
| CompTIA A+ Core 1 (220-1201): Hardware and Mobile Devices sections | Professor Messer (YouTube) | Video series | Free | Required |

**Topics:** Motherboard, CPU, RAM, HDD / SSD, Power supply, GPU, Cooling, Input / output devices, Ports & cables, Compatibility, System architecture

**Skill demonstrations:**

- **Component identification.** Identify motherboard, CPU, RAM, storage, power supply, and expansion components.
- **Component functions.** Explain how major parts support computing tasks.
- **Connections & compatibility.** Recognize connectors and identify basic compatibility constraints.
- **Hardware problem-solving.** Investigate a simple failure and recommend a justified next step.

## 3 | Networking Fundamentals

**Goal:** Understand how networked devices communicate and apply introductory network troubleshooting skills.

| Course / Module | Provider | Access | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Networking Basics | Cisco Networking Academy | Free | Required |
| Introduction to Networking | HTB Academy | Tier 0 / free | Required |
| Introductory Networking | TryHackMe | Free tier | Required |
| Networking Concepts | TryHackMe | Free tier | Required |
| CompTIA A+ Core 1 (220-1201): Networking section | Professor Messer (YouTube) | Free | Required |

**Topics:** LAN / WAN, Switches, Routers, Wireless access points, Network interfaces, Ethernet / Wi-Fi, IP addressing, Subnet masks, Default gateways, DNS, DHCP, Connectivity tests

**Skill demonstrations:**

- **Network components.** Identify the purpose of a switch, router, access point, and network interface.
- **Addressing.** Explain IP address, subnet mask, and default gateway at an introductory level.
- **Connectivity testing.** Use `ping` and a local IP-configuration command and interpret the result.
- **Problem investigation.** Distinguish a likely configuration issue from an observed communication failure.

## 4 | Software & Applications

**Goal:** Understand software types and perform basic application installation, configuration, maintenance, and troubleshooting.

| Course Selection | Access Requirement | Status |
|---|---|---|
| CompTIA A+ Core 2 (220-1202): Operating Systems (application installation) and Software Troubleshooting sections, Professor Messer (YouTube) | Free video instruction | Required |
| IT Essentials (Cisco Networking Academy): Windows installation and configuration, application management, and troubleshooting process modules | Free through a Cisco Academy class (instructor-led) | Required |
| Practical software troubleshooting activities | Free access | In development |

> [!NOTE]
> **Delivery note:** IT Essentials is taught by an instructor through a Cisco Networking Academy. Independent learners without academy access can complete this area with Professor Messer Core 2 plus the practical activities.

**Topics:** System vs. application software, Installation / removal, Compatibility, Updates, Licensing, Configuration, Common errors

**Demonstration:** Install or configure an application in an authorized environment, verify operation, and explain a basic corrective action.

---

## 5 | Troubleshooting & Problem-Solving

**Goal:** Use a repeatable troubleshooting process across hardware, operating systems, applications, and networks.

| Course Selection | Access Requirement | Status |
|---|---|---|
| CompTIA A+ Core 1 (220-1201): Hardware and Network Troubleshooting section, Professor Messer (YouTube) | Free video instruction | Required |
| CompTIA A+ Core 2 (220-1202): Software Troubleshooting section, Professor Messer (YouTube) | Free video instruction | Required |
| Guided troubleshooting practice | Free access | In development |

**The process:** Identify the problem, gather evidence, propose a cause, test, implement a solution, verify, document.

**Demonstration:** Complete a short technical incident report identifying symptoms, tests, findings, resolution, and verification.

## Step 1 Completion Evidence

You have completed Step 1 when you can:

- [ ] Identify core hardware components and explain their functions.
- [ ] Navigate Windows and Linux using basic GUI and command-line tools.
- [ ] Use basic CMD, PowerShell, and Bash commands, and execute a simple Bash script.
- [ ] Explain software configuration, maintenance, and common application problems.
- [ ] Identify network devices, explain basic addressing, and test connectivity.
- [ ] Apply and document a structured troubleshooting process.
- [ ] Complete practical demonstrations without requiring a paid certification exam.
